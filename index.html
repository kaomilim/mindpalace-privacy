Privacy Policy — Mind Palace

Effective date: August 29, 2026
App: Mind Palace (package com.kaomidevstudio.mindpalace)
Developer: Kaomi Dev Studio

Short version: Mind Palace does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, from anyone, including children. The app has no internet access permission and makes zero network calls.

1. Data Collection

Mind Palace collects no data of any kind. The app does not gather personal information, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or any other data about you or your device.

2. Permissions

The only Android permission requested by Mind Palace is android.permission.VIBRATE, used solely to provide haptic feedback during gameplay (for example, a short vibration on certain in-game events). This permission does not grant access to any personal data and cannot be used to collect information.

Mind Palace does not request internet access. The app has no network permission and cannot connect to the internet, so it cannot send data anywhere even if it wanted to.

3. Analytics, Advertising, and Third-Party Services

Mind Palace contains no analytics software development kits (SDKs), no advertising networks, and no third-party libraries of any kind that could collect or transmit data. There are no embedded trackers.

4. Accounts and Sign-In

Mind Palace does not require or support account creation, sign-in, or authentication of any kind. There is no username, password, email, or identity information collected or stored by the app.

5. How Game Progress Is Stored

Your game progress (levels completed, settings, and similar in-game state) is saved only in the app's private local storage on your device (the Godot engine's user:// directory, which is sandboxed to the app by Android). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never transmitted over a network — the app has no internet permission and makes no network calls
  • Is not accessible to Kaomi Dev Studio, to other apps, or to any third party
  • Is removed automatically if you uninstall the app or clear its app data from Android system settings

6. Children's Privacy

Mind Palace is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data whatsoever from any user, it collects no data from children either. There is no mechanism in the app for a child (or anyone) to submit personal information, and none is gathered automatically. This app complies with the intent of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations by design, simply by never collecting anything.

7. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Since no data is collected, no data is shared with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or any other third party. There is nothing to sell, license, or disclose.

8. Security

Because Mind Palace stores information exclusively within the operating system's private, sandboxed app storage and never transmits any data off the device, there is no data-in-transit or server-side storage to secure. Standard Android app sandboxing protects your locally stored game data from other apps.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that involves data collection — this page will be updated with a new effective date, and the change will be described here before it takes effect. We recommend checking back periodically if you have concerns.
